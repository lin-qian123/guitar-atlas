# Guitar Atlas (六弦漫行) third-party notices

Guitar Atlas is an independent multi-source catalog and local offline library.
It is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by the websites below.
Source records retain their own identity and attribution, even when titles
look alike or local files contain identical bytes.

The following 16 sources match `config/sources.json`. Original source titles,
attributions and identifiers remain available alongside project-authored
display translations and normalized catalog fields. The source observations
below follow the frozen acquisition ledger and research receipts; they are not
a new grant of rights or a source-wide copyright determination.

## IMSLP / Petrucci Music Library

[IMSLP](https://imslp.org/) supplies work and instrumentation-category records.
The catalog preserves original titles, musician attributions, native work IDs,
category names, and links to the corresponding work and category pages.

The project is not affiliated with Project Petrucci LLC or IMSLP mirror
operators. IMSLP page content and individual files have their own applicable
terms. Copyright status can differ by jurisdiction; the catalog's inclusion
rules do not constitute a copyright determination.

See IMSLP's [general disclaimer](https://imslp.org/wiki/IMSLP:General_disclaimer)
and [licensing policy](https://imslp.org/wiki/IMSLP:Free_content_licenses).

## ClassClef

[ClassClef](https://www.classclef.com/) supplies guitar repertoire records and
source-directory relationships. The catalog preserves available original titles,
musician attributions, source record IDs, and links to source pages. Source
classification is kept separate from IMSLP's exact instrumentation categories.

ClassClef availability or a free-download label does not establish a score's
copyright status or grant redistribution permission. Any attribution, edition,
arranger, copyright, and licensing information in the source page or file remains
applicable. Guitar Atlas does not claim ownership of ClassClef content or extend
its own license to source files.

## Mutopia

[Mutopia](https://www.mutopiaproject.org/) supplies the official Guitar directory
and individual piece records. Original credits and each edition's stated
license remain applicable; a license observed on one record does not cover
every Mutopia edition. Public records link to source pages rather than files.

## The Guitar School

[The Guitar School](https://classicalguitarschool.azurewebsites.net/) supplies
catalog and item records, including free and paid editions. The local acquisition
scope is limited to explicitly licensed free PDFs; record-specific Creative
Commons conditions, including noncommercial conditions where stated, remain
separate from this project's license. A catalog link grants no access to paid files.

## Classical Guitar Library

[Classical Guitar Library](https://www.cglib.org/) supplies composer-index and
CMS records. Its stated site-metadata [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)
notice is preserved with source attribution; Guitar Atlas adds translations and
normalized display fields. Individual score editions retain their own rights
notices, and the metadata notice does not authorize redistribution of those files.

## Delcamp

[Delcamp](https://www.classical-guitar-sheet-music.com/) supplies edition and
collection-directory records. Its source editions state personal noncommercial
use conditions and redistribution restrictions; local availability does not
extend those conditions or authorize a public mirror. Directory relationships
and original credits remain attributed to Delcamp.

## Andrew York

[Andrew York](https://andrewyork.net/) supplies the author's repertoire and
product catalog. Public links retain the source titles and credits; a zero-price
listing does not establish an open license. The catalog grants no checkout,
file-acquisition or score-redistribution rights.

## Guitar Downunder

[Guitar Downunder](https://www.guitardownunder.com/) supplies classical,
fingerstyle and ensemble-directory records. The recorded homepage notice is
All Rights Reserved; external metadata or file reuse beyond source-attributed
navigation has not been established. Public directory links confer no file-copying rights.

## ClassicalGuitar.org

[ClassicalGuitar.org](https://www.classicalguitar.org/) supplies its Free Stuff
directory, including score and reference-material entries. Its
[site policies](https://www.classicalguitar.org/about/site-policies/) distinguish
articles, original compositions, public-domain material and purchased products;
the label "free" does not replace the applicable conditions for each item.

## FreeGuitarMusic.Net

[FreeGuitarMusic.Net](https://www.freeguitarmusic.net/) supplies named entries
from its modern and classical fingerstyle directory. File permissions and
external-viewer access were not established by the directory discovery;
Guitar Atlas provides source-page navigation without granting copying or
redistribution rights to the linked scores.

## Cantorion

[Cantorion](https://cantorion.org/) supplies the accessible Guitar search records
and their original credits. Rights are stated per record, including reserved-rights
editions; a mirrored Mutopia edition retains its own credit and license rather
than acquiring a new blanket license. Pagination access limits remain a coverage boundary.

## Library of Congress

[Library of Congress](https://www.loc.gov/) supplies notated-music item metadata
and institutional source-page links. Rights and access statements belong to the
individual item and collection; an observed freely reusable collection does not
establish the status of every Library of Congress item or scan. This catalog
grants no additional rights to source files.

## Boije Collection

[Boije Collection](https://old.capricemusic.se/musikochteaterbiblioteket/ladda-ner-noter/boijes-samling/)
supplies its official alphabetical historical-collection index and native
collection numbers. Institutional credits and publication conditions remain
applicable. The integration is directory navigation; advertised PDF availability
does not grant permission to copy or republish scans or bypass access restrictions.

## Digital Guitar Archive

[Digital Guitar Archive](https://digitalguitararchive.com/archive/) supplies
historical bibliographic records through its documented legacy API. Its database
republication license remains unspecified in the frozen evidence; the API's
availability is not a redistribution license. Institutional holding references
and source links grant no acquisition or republication rights to scans held elsewhere.

## RISM

[RISM](https://rism.online/) supplies musical-source, musician and institutional
metadata through its official interfaces. The recorded metadata license is
[CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/); source attribution and
original identifiers are retained alongside project translations and normalized
fields. This metadata license does not cover scans or other material at linked
holding institutions, and file acquisition is outside this integration's scope.

## This is Classical Guitar / Werner

[This is Classical Guitar / Werner](https://www.thisisclassicalguitar.com/)
supplies repertoire and collection-page records with available original credits.
The recorded free-collection sample states All Rights Reserved; free availability
does not establish a blanket reuse license. Source-page and editor-shop references
grant no score-copying or redistribution rights.

## Publication boundary

The public site hosts no scores, MIDI, or Guitar Pro files and exposes no direct
file or download URLs. Verified files in a private local collection are not
included in the Git repository or GitHub Pages export. Every public record links
to a validated page on its source website.

New sources must be listed in the source registry and documented here before
their records are published. Their source terms remain separate from the
project's software and original documentation licenses.

The source-specific scope and evidence are recorded in
[`config/source_acquisition_policies.json`](config/source_acquisition_policies.json),
[`docs/research/2026-10-01-source-expansion.md`](docs/research/2026-10-01-source-expansion.md)
and [`docs/research/2026-10-01-integration-log.md`](docs/research/2026-10-01-integration-log.md).
The candidate ledger also contains unregistered research candidates; those are
not additional published sources or file-acquisition authorization.
